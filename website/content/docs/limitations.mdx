---
title: Not yet implemented
description: What Loupe does not do yet, as of v0.1.7.
icon: Construction
---

As of v0.1.7:

- **Creating objects.** Loupe edits what already exists; there is no
  *New*, and no apply from a local file.
- **Schema-aware editing.** The YAML editor has no completion and no
  validation before the apply round-trip.
- **Metrics-based problems.** [Problems](/docs/problems) does not detect
  CPU throttling or other conditions that need metrics over time, and
  sends no notifications or alerts.
- **Authenticode signing on Windows.** The installers are unsigned, so
  SmartScreen warns on first run.

The [issue tracker](https://github.com/kryptonhq/loupe/issues) has
what is planned.
