---
title: Detail views
description: Pods, nodes and namespaces have views of their own; every object has its events and manifest.
icon: FileSearch
---

- **Pod** — containers and their images, termination reasons,
  conditions, labels, events and the full manifest, with
  [logs](/docs/logs-and-exec), a shell and a port forward a tab away
- **Node** — allocated CPU and memory against allocatable, taints,
  pressure conditions, and the pods bound to the node
- **Namespace** — pod phase counts, ResourceQuota utilisation,
  remaining finalizers on a terminating namespace, and the resources it
  contains

Every other kind opens on an overview, with tabs for what applies to
it:

- **Data** — ConfigMaps and Secrets, key by key
- **Logs** — workloads with a selector, merged across their pods
- **Events** — namespaced objects
- **Related** — owners, selections, routes and references; see
  [related objects](/docs/browsing#related-objects)
- **YAML** — the manifest, with [editing](/docs/editing)

![Pod detail: containers, conditions, a port forward and labels](/img/loupe/pod-detail.png)

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes a detail view and returns to the listing it came
from.
